# Capitology Training — Ultimate Timeline + Distance Rule

Human correction from Fight 4 (~42:08 onward).

## Required analysis procedure
For every serious fight analysis:
1. Build an accurate chronological timeline of every ultimate used.
2. Identify which ultimates are proactive and which are direct responses to another ultimate/action.
3. Record the approximate distance / space between teams when each ultimate is activated.
4. Evaluate how that distance changes the ultimate's value, timing, and available enemy responses.
5. Only then infer the fight plan and correction.

Do not summarize a fight as "Team A used more ults" without understanding the response chain.

## Why distance matters
Ultimate value is not independent of geometry.
Examples to test in every fight:
- A forward-engage ultimate used while teams are still far apart may spend much of its duration simply closing distance.
- A defensive/sustain ultimate used while there is still space may let a team kite, disengage, or absorb the engage without becoming trapped.
- An ultimate that creates or forces close-range contact can radically change the value of later ults.
- Some counter-ults become stronger specifically because the first ult compresses the fight into their preferred range/shape.
