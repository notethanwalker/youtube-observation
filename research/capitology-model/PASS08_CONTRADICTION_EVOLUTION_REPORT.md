# Pass 8 — contradictions and evolution

**Status: adjudication complete for the ten highest-value apparent tensions; empirical limits remain open.** This is an account of Capitology's stated model across the known uploads, not a proof that the strategies won for the reasons he gives. `pass08_adjudications.jsonl` holds the claim IDs, publication dates, exact time links, visual-window links, classifications, findings, and open tests for each case. `pass08_adjudications.py` validates those references and reproduces the ledger.

## Method and evidence boundary

I compared the 206 Pass 4 paraphrased claims, the 16 Pass 5 concepts and 12 proposed relationships, and all 23 Pass 7 visual observations. The ten cases below draw on 56 distinct claim anchors and, where relevant, all 23 selected windows. I reread timed caption intervals for the decisive definitions, caveats, follow-up, and possible reversals. Claims about what a player saw, why an opponent chose a route, or which counterfactual would win remain the speaker's interpretation unless the sampled video directly shows the observable part. Several clips pause, annotate, or switch POV, and the audio was not retained in the 480p windows.

The date is a **YouTube upload date**, not a verified match, recording, patch, or first-expression date. A later upload can elaborate an old idea without documenting when the speaker changed his mind. The ledger is a deliberately selected set of propositions, so claim counts and the ten adjudications are not estimates of how often he used an idea. Some 2026 team analysis may have moved to a separate venue, as the Pass 4 announcement notes; absence from the public channel does not establish abandonment. No high-visual Pass 4 claim has been automatically promoted to `gameplay_verified`.

Classification means one of: a definition became more operational; different conditions or stages explain an apparent opposition; a matchup creates an exception; an example was explicitly corrected; or the empirical comparison remains untested. **No direct, unconditional reversal is demonstrated in these ten cases.** This does not prove that the full speech corpus contains none.

The strongest continuity candidate is a **useful next response**: space, sight, objective pressure, and a first resource exchange shape the opponent's options, while the team preserves a way to answer the chosen response. It is a synthesis hypothesis spanning turns, bait, retreat, dive prevention, splits, and ultimate conversion. Pass 9 should test it against role-specific and composition-specific claims rather than make it a universal law. Mechanics, attention, learning method, and design opinions remain distinct parts of the corpus; the six visual themes do not subsume them.
